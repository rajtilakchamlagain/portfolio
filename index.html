Technology Innovation Hub (IIT Guwahati)

Academic Research Intern

The Problem: In standard Video Person Re-Identification (Re-ID), police systems rely heavily on manual "Threshold Guessing". In blurry, dark CCTV footage, this human bias often leads to catastrophic false positives.

The Solution (ClearSight AI): I engineered an Autonomous Spectral Gap Engine. Instead of relying on a human, the algorithm sorts the cosine similarity vectors of everyone in the crowd, calculates the maximal first-derivative drop-off, and autonomously places a dynamic, zero-shot threshold gate.

Future Scope: Upgrading backend to handle live RTSP camera feeds and integrating FAISS vector databases to index millions of identities in milliseconds.

Supervised by Dr. Mahapara Khursid.
